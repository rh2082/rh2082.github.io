title: About Me
---

I am a PhD student studying solar eruptive phenomena, mainly focussed on flares, at UCL/MSSL. I'm particularly interested in EUV imaging and solar X-rays, and mainly use Solar Orbiter observations in my research. I also have a keen interest in space weather, particularly forecasting.

I did an undergraduate degree in BSc Physics (Hons) at the University of Bath from 2021 to 2024. I graduated with a first. I was also Chair of Bath Space Society in my final year, along with volunteering as a theatre and live event technician with Backstage Technical Services. I absolutely loved both of those experiences. After that, I studied an MSc in Mathematical and Theoretical Physics at the University of Sheffield, from which I graduated with a Distinction. While studying my MSc, I attended BSL classes and hope to continue learning BSL. I started my PhD three weeks after submitting my master's dissertation, which was on Modelling Active Regions with Magnetic Flux Tubes and Non-Linear Force-Free Magnetic Fields.

I endeavour to be involved in outreach and have given a couple of talks. For example, in October 2024, I gave a talk on "How to Build a Solar Storm" to Sheffield Astronomical Society.
