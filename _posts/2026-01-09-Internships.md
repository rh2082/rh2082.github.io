title: Internships for Undergrads
---

Here is a list of primarily physics internships (but there are some other disciplines) in a range of locations, generally aimed for undergraduate students seeking research experience.

<h3><a href="https://leaps.strw.leidenuniv.nl/">The Leiden/ESA Astrophysics Program for Summer Students (LEAPS)</a></h3>
<p>Summary: 10 week research project at Leiden Observatory or ESA/ESTEC. Students are provided with travel costs, housing accommodations, a stipend to help with living costs and health insurance. </p>

<h3><a href="https://www.daad.de/rise/en/rise-germany/">Research Internships in Science and Engineering (RISE)</a></h3>
<p>Summary: Research projects for undergraduate students from North American, British and Irish universities. Students are matched with an institution according to their area of interest, such as biology, chemistry, physics, earth sciences, engineering or similar. The project is up to three months, and students will receive a monthly scholarship to help cover living costs and international travel costs. Health insurance, accident and personal liability insurance are provided. </p>

<h3><a href="https://www.mitacs.ca/our-programs/globalink-research-internship-students/">Globalink Research Internship</a></h3>
<p>12 week research project offered to undergraduate students in Mitac’s partner countries. Based in Canada, with subjects such as science, engineering, mathematics, humanities and social sciences. Funding (dependent on country of origin and host institution) covers cost of round-trip to Canada, transport from airport to accommodation, housing, enrolment fees, food, health insurance. There is also support from student mentors, access to industry events and development courses.</p>

<h3><a href="https://cosmicorigins.space/cassum">Chalmers Astrophysics & Space Science Summer (CASSUM) Research Fellowships</a></h3>
<p>Summary: 10 week research project mostly online, with remote supervision. Most supervisors are at Chalmers University in Sweden, with students expected to visit for at least part of this time. Students receive a stipend for participation, as well as full travel and housing support for their visit.</p>

<h3><a href="https://www.astron.nl/education/summer-research-programme/">ASTRON/JIVE Summer Research Programme</a></h3>
<p>Summary: Offered by the Netherlands Institute for Radio Astronomy and Joint Institute for VLBI ERIC. 10-12 weeks conducting astronomical studies in the Netherlands, in topics such as observational cosmology, stellar maser astrometry and cosmic magnetism. Students have a stipend for up to three months, and full travel reimbursement. </p>

<h3><a href="https://summerstudents.desy.de/">DESY Summer Students Program</a></h3>
<p>Summary: 8 week research project in photon science, elementary particle physics and accelerators or astroparticle physics. Selected candidates join in the day-to-day work of research groups at the DESY Laboratory in Hamburg or Zeuthen (Berlin). The program also includes a series of lectures, visits to experiments, accelerators and computer centres. Students receive a salary for their 39 hours of work per week. There is partial travel reimbursement and hostel accommodation is provided for free. </p>

<h3><a href="https://eso.org/sci/meetings/2026/SummerResearch2026.html">European Southern Observatory Research Programme</a></h3>
<p>Summary: 6 week research programme based at ESO in Garching, Germany.  The projects cover a range of topics from planets to galaxies. Students will be able to attend a lecture series, workshop, planetarium and ELT visit.  Travel costs to and from Garching will be covered, accommodation will be provided, and a stipend is provided for living costs. Students are expected to acquire any needed visas. </p>

<h3><a href="https://www.mpia.de/en/careers/internships/summer">Student Summer Internship 2026 at the Max Planck Institute for Astronomy (MPIA)</a></h3>
<p>Summary: Two to three month research programme, in topics such as exoplanets, Milky Way and nearby galaxies, and quasar detection. Travel costs are supported and a salary is. The internship typically runs between May and September. Students from developing countries are strongly encouraged to apply as some positions are reserved for them</p>

<h3><a href="https://www.esa.int/About_Us/Careers_at_ESA/Student_Internships2">ESA Student Internships</a></h3>
<p>Summary: Three to six month internships for students in their penultimate or final year of a Master’s degree. Disciplines include engineering, product assurance, mathematics, life sciences, law, finance and more. A monthly allowance is granted, with the amount dependent on their living distance from the relevant establishment. Students are responsible for their own health insurance, accident insurance, travel expenses and finding accommodation.</p>

<h3><a href="https://tssp.umk.pl/exsci/">TSSP ExSci 2025 - Toruń Students Summer Program in Exact Sciences</a></h3>
<p>Summary: 4 week summer research project in astronomy, physics, mathematics, informatics, automatics, robotics or computer science. The projects are generally based at the Nicolaus Copernicus University, and students will be invited to weekly lectures and lunch/dinner. Students are offered student housing and a daily allowance. </p>


<h3><a href="https://careers.cern/programmes/summer-studentship/">CERN Summer Student Programme</a></h3>
<p>Summary: An advanced technical project over 8-13 weeks, including a series of lectures. There are also visits to CERN, discussion sessions and workshops. A daily allowance, travel allowance, health insurance and assistance to find accommodation is provided.</p>

<h3><a href="https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/internships/eligibility">Philippa Fawcett Internship Programme</a></h3>
<p>Summary: A funded summer research programme for women, trans women and non-binary students in mathematics, based at the University of Cambridge. The internship runs for 8 weeks between July and September. Interns are paid the National Living Wage, plus holiday pay. Accommodation is provided at one of the Cambridge colleges, but the cost is not covered by the programme.</p>
