---
title: Internships for Undergrads
---

Here is a list of primarily physics internships (but there are some other disciplines) in a range of locations, generally aimed at undergraduate students seeking research experience.

<h3><a href="https://leaps.strw.leidenuniv.nl/">The Leiden/ESA Astrophysics Program for Summer Students (LEAPS)</a></h3>
<p>10 week research project at Leiden Observatory or ESA/ESTEC. Students are provided with travel costs, housing accommodations, a stipend to help with living costs and health insurance. </p>

<h3><a href="https://www.daad.de/rise/en/rise-germany/">Research Internships in Science and Engineering (RISE)</a></h3>
<p>Research projects for undergraduate students from North American, British and Irish universities. Students are matched with an institution according to their area of interest, such as biology, chemistry, physics, earth sciences, engineering or similar. The project is up to three months, and students will receive a monthly scholarship to help cover living costs and international travel costs. Health insurance, accident and personal liability insurance are provided. </p>

<h3><a href="https://www.mitacs.ca/our-programs/globalink-research-internship-students/">Globalink Research Internship</a></h3>
<p>12 week research project offered to undergraduate students in Mitac’s partner countries. Based in Canada, with subjects such as science, engineering, mathematics, humanities and social sciences. Funding (dependent on country of origin and host institution) covers cost of round-trip to Canada, transport from airport to accommodation, housing, enrolment fees, food, health insurance. There is also support from student mentors, access to industry events and development courses.</p>

<h3><a href="https://cosmicorigins.space/cassum">Chalmers Astrophysics & Space Science Summer (CASSUM) Research Fellowships</a></h3>
<p>10 week research project mostly online, with remote supervision. Most supervisors are at Chalmers University in Sweden, with students expected to visit for at least part of this time. Students receive a stipend for participation, as well as full travel and housing support for their visit.</p>

<h3><a href="https://www.astron.nl/education/summer-research-programme/">ASTRON/JIVE Summer Research Programme</a></h3>
<p>Offered by the Netherlands Institute for Radio Astronomy and Joint Institute for VLBI ERIC. 10-12 weeks conducting astronomical studies in the Netherlands, in topics such as observational cosmology, stellar maser astrometry and cosmic magnetism. Students have a stipend for up to three months, and full travel reimbursement. </p>

<h3><a href="https://summerstudents.desy.de/">DESY Summer Students Program</a></h3>
<p>8 week research project in photon science, elementary particle physics and accelerators or astroparticle physics. Selected candidates join in the day-to-day work of research groups at the DESY Laboratory in Hamburg or Zeuthen (Berlin). The program also includes a series of lectures, visits to experiments, accelerators and computer centres. Students receive a salary for their 39 hours of work per week. There is partial travel reimbursement and hostel accommodation is provided for free. </p>

<h3><a href="https://eso.org/sci/meetings/2026/SummerResearch2026.html">European Southern Observatory Research Programme</a></h3>
<p>6 week research programme based at ESO in Garching, Germany.  The projects cover a range of topics from planets to galaxies. Students will be able to attend a lecture series, workshop, planetarium and ELT visit.  Travel costs to and from Garching will be covered, accommodation will be provided, and a stipend is provided for living costs. Students are expected to acquire any needed visas. </p>

<h3><a href="https://www.mpia.de/en/careers/internships/summer">Student Summer Internship 2026 at the Max Planck Institute for Astronomy (MPIA)</a></h3>
<p>Two to three month research programme, in topics such as exoplanets, Milky Way and nearby galaxies, and quasar detection. Travel costs are supported and a salary is. The internship typically runs between May and September. Students from developing countries are strongly encouraged to apply as some positions are reserved for them</p>

<h3><a href="https://www.esa.int/About_Us/Careers_at_ESA/Student_Internships2">ESA Student Internships</a></h3>
<p>Three to six month internships for students in their penultimate or final year of a Master’s degree. Disciplines include engineering, product assurance, mathematics, life sciences, law, finance and more. A monthly allowance is granted, with the amount dependent on their living distance from the relevant establishment. Students are responsible for their own health insurance, accident insurance, travel expenses and finding accommodation.</p>

<h3><a href="https://tssp.umk.pl/exsci/">TSSP ExSci 2025 - Toruń Students Summer Program in Exact Sciences</a></h3>
<p>4 week summer research project in astronomy, physics, mathematics, informatics, automatics, robotics or computer science. The projects are generally based at the Nicolaus Copernicus University, and students will be invited to weekly lectures and lunch/dinner. Students are offered student housing and a daily allowance. </p>


<h3><a href="https://careers.cern/programmes/summer-studentship/">CERN Summer Student Programme</a></h3>
<p>An advanced technical project over 8-13 weeks, including a series of lectures. There are also visits to CERN, discussion sessions and workshops. A daily allowance, travel allowance, health insurance and assistance to find accommodation is provided.</p>

<h3><a href="https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/internships/eligibility">Philippa Fawcett Internship Programme</a></h3>
<p>A funded summer research programme for women, trans women and non-binary students in mathematics, based at the University of Cambridge. The internship runs for 8 weeks between July and September. Interns are paid the National Living Wage, plus holiday pay. Accommodation is provided at one of the Cambridge colleges, but the cost is not covered by the programme.</p>

<h3><a href="https://www.physics.ox.ac.uk/research/summer-internships-undergraduates">Oxford Summer Research Program</a></h3>
<p>The selected students will work with a supervisor in the department, usually a postdoctoral researcher or lecturer, on a self-contained research project typically lasting 8 weeks. These are run by the research groups in astrophysics; atomic and laser physics and atmospheric, oceanic and planetary physics. For astrophysics projects the programme will also include lectures/seminars on current astrophysics topics, and on academic careers in astro. Students will be encouraged to take part in department life, joining researchers for coffee, discussions and seminars, and are paid as employees of the university. </p>

<h3><a href="https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/access/uniq-plus?source=access">UNIQ+ Projects</a></h3>
<p>A seven-week research programme for undergraduates from under-represented groups, who would find continuing into postgraduate study a challenge for reasons other than their academic ability. Project topics cover medical sciences; mathematical, physical and life sciences; humanities or social sciences. </p>

<h3><a href="https://www.qub.ac.uk/research-centres/astrophysics-research-centre/EducationandOpportunities/Internships/">University of Belfast Summer Studentships</a></h3>
<p>Paid summer internship positions to work on research projects in topics such as solar system, the sun and exoplanets. The studentships are expected to be 8 weeks.</p>

<h3><a href="https://careers.ukaea.uk/early-careers/placements-and-work-experience/">UK Atomic Energy Authority Placements</a></h3>
<p>Offer both summer and industrial placements to give students chance to experience the fusion industry.</p>


<h3><a href="https://www.herts.ac.uk/research/centres/car/ug-research-programme">University of Hertfordshire Undergraduate Research Programme</a></h3>
<p>Projects designed to give undergraduates a feel for what research is like. They are in the Department of Physics, Astronomy and Mathematics and include topics such as climate modelling, astrophysics and programming. Some limited funding may be available.</p>

<h3><a href="https://stfccareers.co.uk/students/">Science and Technology Facilities Council Placements</a></h3>
<p>Offer summer and industrial placements in engineering, computing, science and technology, business support, finance and microelectronics.</p>

<h3><a href="https://in2scienceuk.org/our-programmes/in2research/">Science and Technology Facilities Council Placements</a></h3>
<p>8 week research placement designed to enhance access to postgraduate research degrees and career opportunities for people from low socioeconomic backgrounds and under-represented groups. Includes online workshops, mentorship and away days. Students will be supported by a stipend.</p>

<h3><a href="https://prosperofellowship.org/">Prospero Space Fellowships</a></h3>
<p>3-8+ week summer internships, aimed for students interested in the space industry. Students are matched with a partner organisation.</p>

<h3><a href="https://sa.catapult.org.uk/spin/">Space Placements in INdustry (SPIN)</a></h3>
<p>Space Placements in Industry offers short term research placements and is managed by the UK Space Agency. The internships are typically 8 weeks long, and are paid. Includes an induction event at the start and a poster and showcase event at the end.</p>


<h3><a href="https://www.aei.mpg.de/43630/student-internships-in-gravitational-wave-searches-and-observations">Student Internships at the Albert Einstein Institute</a></h3>
<p>Summer internships in the “Observational Relativity and Cosmology” department at the Max Planck institute. Research topics include gravitational wave astronomy using laser interferometers and pulsar timing arrays. </p>

<h3><a href="https://aspire.science.uva.nl/">Astrophysics Summer Program for International Research Experience (ASPIRE)</a></h3>
<p>An 8 week astronomical research programme at the University of Amsterdam. Topics include exoplanets, extreme astrophysics and black holes. There is also a weekly series of lectures covering topics such as programming and research skills. Travel, lodging, subsistence and visas are funded.</p>

<h3><a href="https://www.imperial.ac.uk/urop/">Undergraduate Research Opportunities Programme</a></h3>
<p>A research internship offered by Imperial College London. Applications are done by reaching out to participating members of staff regarding possible projects. Topics include bioengineering, mathematics, earth sciences, chemistry, physics and more. Bursaries can be provided.</p>
